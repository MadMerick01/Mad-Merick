# What's changed

Every change to Mad Merick, in plain English, newest first. Each build is a new download on the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page.

## Coming next

Changes that are finished but not in a download yet. They move into a numbered build as soon as it's published.

- **The first playable skeleton of Mad Merick.** Press **Start the run** to be teleported to Port Wakefield, at the edge of the desert north of Adelaide, and head north for Pine Gap. There is one run and one save, with no restarts.
- **The HUD strip** shows the threat light, the fuel in your tank, six ammo pips for the revolver and the road distance left to Pine Gap. Click it to open the **Road** and **Rig** tabs. The ammo pips stay dark until the revolver gets ammo in a later update.
- **Save here** keeps where you are and how much fuel you have. You can't save with Hoons nearby. Next time you play, **Go to your save** takes you back and puts your tank back as it was.
- **In VR, a wrist HUD** on your left hand shows the same strip, drawn from simple shapes like the revolver so it doesn't lag.
- **Every update is now a download.** Each new version appears on the Releases page as `mad-merick.zip`. Copy it into your BeamNG `mods` folder without extracting it, replacing the old one.
